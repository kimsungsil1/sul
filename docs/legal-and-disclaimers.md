# Legal & Disclaimers

## Purpose
The Birth-Year Liquor Recommender is an informational recommendation tool. It does not sell alcohol directly. All purchases occur via external retailers.

## Age gate
- Korean legal drinking age: **19+**.
- The app uses an age gate at entry and requires confirmation before viewing recommendations.

## Disclaimers (shown in UI)
- Recommendations are informational and may not reflect real-time availability.
- Prices and availability can change; always verify on retailer sites.
- Drink responsibly.

## Regional restrictions (high level)
- Availability and shipping restrictions vary by region and retailer.
- Users should verify local laws and retailer policies before purchase.

## Data policy
- No scraping of prohibited sources.
- Connectors use official APIs, affiliate feeds, or open datasets with clear licensing.
